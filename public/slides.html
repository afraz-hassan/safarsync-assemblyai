Commercial truck drivers and independent owner-operators are forced to choose between road safety and manual bookkeeping. Current software forces drivers to type logs onto tiny smartphone touchscreens inside moving truck cabins.

Lost Fuel & Tax Write-Offs

The Solution

SafarSync AI: 100% Hands-Free Telematics

Drivers simply speak naturally into their phone or truck dashboard. The app captures live voice, parses numbers and units, and updates operational databases in real time.
